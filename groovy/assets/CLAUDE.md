# GroovyScript asset overlay

`groovy/assets/<modid>/{lang,models,textures}/...` ships custom lang, model and texture resources without a separate resourcepack: the lang, model and texture of the `aeternum:` items registered in `groovy/preInit/items.groovy` (`copper_chipset`, `compressed_electrotine`, `compressed_glowstone`, `quicksilver_dust`), the Requious JEI tab titles, and the Mekanism infuse types (`infuse.<name>` lang keys, icons under `textures/blocks/infuse/`). Textures here are also stitched into the block atlas, so a `ResourceLocation` like `aeternum:blocks/infuse/electrotine` works for anything that registers its own sprite. Lang keys merge globally, whichever folder or modid declares them. `content.createItem` gives no ore-dict entry; add one with `oreDict.add(...)` in the postInit script that uses the item (`dustQuicksilver` in `groovy/postInit/mods/thaumcraft.groovy`).
